# Make your own mode skill

A mode skill holds what one kind of work needs every time: its sources, its voice, its standing rules. The first Bodhi grew several, one for each area of its founder's work. They are not shipped, because they were one person's. Write yours only after you have re-explained the same context three times.

Copy this folder into your harness's skills folder as `bodhi-<mode>`, change `name` above to match, rewrite `description`, and fill in each section. Delete what you do not need.
