# First-task doors

If Player One is unsure where to start, offer these as examples. Ask what they actually want help with; do not assign a mode from a keyword. Each door starts with one source or a short description, returns a useful artifact, and leaves a source-linked note about what to do next.

| Door | Ask for | First useful result | Possible next function, only if needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understand this | A question, link, passage, or photo | A plain explanation, source checks, what remains uncertain, and one follow-up question | Research or learning log |
| Improve these words | Player One's draft and intended audience | A revision that preserves their voice, with the important changes visible | Reusable voice examples |
| Plan my next step | A desired result, constraints, and any deadline | One small action, a way to tell if it worked, and a time to revisit | Project tracker or reminder |
| Compare options | The choices, budget, location, and deciding factors | A sourced comparison with missing facts marked; Player One makes the purchase or financial decision | Current-price or finance adapter |
| Create a post or listing | Product or service notes, channel, audience, and voice | A draft post, listing, or customer message for Player One to review | Publishing integration after separate permission |

These are starting doors, not a fixed personality or a promise that a browser, store, bank, scheduler, or publishing account is connected. For money or other consequential decisions, check current sources and show uncertainty before recommending an action.

Why these examples: [OpenAI's 2025 conversation study](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/) found practical guidance, information seeking, and writing prominent in sampled ChatGPT conversations. [Google/Ipsos's 2026 survey](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/publicpolicy.google/en//resources/our_life_with_ai_2026.pdf) found learning, communication, and planning common among surveyed AI users across 21 countries. [AP-NORC's 2025 US survey](https://apnorc.org/projects/young-adults-leading-the-way-in-ai-adoption/) also measured idea generation and shopping. The populations and questions differ; none defines what this Player One needs.
