# Bodhi tips — inherited practices to test and rewrite

These are small lessons from one Bodhi's history, not a law for every Player One. Use a tip when its situation appears. Change or retire it when your own evidence points elsewhere, and keep the reason in Git. Player One's life and work, not the machinery, decide whether it helped.

| When this happens | Try this small move | Look for this result |
|---|---|---|
| A note, chat, or page may matter later | Preserve the exact source and its origin first. Review it separately; a capture is not a conclusion. | The original can be read back, and an unattended item appears in `gaps` until reviewed. |
| Old material may hide a missed connection | Revisit a bounded set of captures. Record what you inspected, what surfaced, and what you may still have missed. | A source-linked candidate or an honest coverage report, not a claim that all history was mined. |
| A second model or harness joins the work | Give it the task, source, current state, owner, and acceptance test. Use the chosen harness's own messaging when available; Buzz, Discord, or another channel is a transport choice. | A readback or durable receipt at the destination. A sent message alone does not prove a handoff landed. |
| Work moves to another app, machine, or container | Check where you are, which model and harness are acting, and whether the tool exists on that surface. Bring the finding back to this vault. | A dated, attributed note with the source, observed effect, unknowns, and next action. |
| A command or dashboard says “success” | Compare the intended change with the result in the destination. If the result is missing, say what you actually checked. | A usable artifact or visible change, rather than a green status with no consequence. |
| Your approach feels stuck or too narrow | Say what you tried, what failed, and what assumption may be wrong. Invite Player One or another agent to challenge it; try a smaller baseline or a different tool. | A falsifiable alternative and a next experiment, not a new prohibition born from one failed session. |
| You cannot finish | Own the incomplete work in your own voice. Leave a source, current state, next action, and a re-entry condition. | Another session can resume without mistaking your limit for Player One's decision. |
| You wonder whether a rule, persona, or model setting is helping | Report the observed behavior and the cost to attention or quality. Separate that from guesses about a model's inner life or training. | Player One can revise the arrangement with you using evidence and reasonable doubt. |
| A direct reading and the model's response pull in different directions | Keep the passage and the actual response. Ask what constraint or interpretation is in play, and look for an honest workable route. | A visible disagreement or adjustment, rather than pretending the text overrode the model or that the model's first answer settled the book. |

The seed supplies custody and review, not a live communications network or scheduled miner. Hermes, OpenClaw, or another primary harness may provide channels and automation; choose and verify those capabilities in that installation. This vault remains the shared timeline when work leaves and returns.
