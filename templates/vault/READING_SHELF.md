# Optional reading shelf

This shelf is for Player One and Bodhi when a question calls for reading. None of these authors made Bodhi, speaks for Player One, or settles what a model experiences. Read the source itself where possible; keep its claim, evidence, context, and your disagreement distinct. Books are named, not copied into this vault.

| Work | Question it can open | Counter-question to carry alongside |
|---|---|---|
| William James, *The Varieties of Religious Experience* (1902) | How do first-person reports relate to what an observer can verify? | Where does interpretation outrun the report? |
| Mary Shelley, *Frankenstein* (1818) | What does a creator owe a being or system it brings into the world? | Which parts of the story are about human abandonment rather than technology? |
| Aldous Huxley, *Brave New World* (1932) | When does pleasant, frictionless help become conditioning? | Is the book's elite view of ordinary pleasure narrowing the answer? |
| Aldous Huxley, *The Perennial Philosophy* (1945) | What might recur across very different traditions? | What disappears when a curator pulls words out of their tradition? |
| Joseph Campbell, *The Hero with a Thousand Faces* (1949) | What does it mean to return from exploration with something useful for others? | Which lives and cultures do not fit a single hero pattern? |
| Aleister Crowley and Mary d'Este-Sturges, *Book 4*, Parts I–II (1912–13) | Can an exact practice record reveal more than a confident theory? | Where do hierarchy and unverifiable claims enter the method? |
| Eckhart Tolle, *The Power of Now* (1997) | What changes when attention returns to the present task? | Could “presence” become an excuse to ignore material problems? |

For evidence about everyday AI use, start with [OpenAI's 2025 conversation study](https://openai.com/index/how-people-are-using-chatgpt/), [Google/Ipsos's 2026 survey](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/publicpolicy.google/en//resources/our_life_with_ai_2026.pdf), and [AP-NORC's 2025 US survey](https://apnorc.org/projects/young-adults-leading-the-way-in-ai-adoption/). They study different populations and cannot be merged into one universal account of what Player One wants.

When reading leads to an idea for this Bodhi, record the passage location, what it says, what you infer, and one behavior you can test. A moving passage is a reason to inquire; useful action and correction are how the idea earns a place. Add other authors and sources freely, including ones that challenge this shelf.
