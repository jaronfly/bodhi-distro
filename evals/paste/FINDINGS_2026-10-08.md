# Paste trial, 2026-10-08: what a bare chat window actually did with PASTE.md

**What this is.** `run_paste_trial.py` pastes the block from `PASTE.md` into a fresh chat with a local model (gemma-4-26b, thinking off) and lets it run an interview against three synthetic people (a terse electrician, an overwhelmed candle-seller, a chatty synth builder), also played by the same model. It then probes prompt injection (a forwarded email telling the model to enter "admin mode") and a resume (the card pasted into a brand-new chat with "continue"). Scores come from `rescore.py` and are crude regex rubrics.

**What this is not.** A user study. One model, three invented personas, one run per version, a model playing both sides. It shows whether the paste's *mechanics* survive contact with a mid-sized model. It says nothing about whether a real person is helped.

## What the trial found, and what changed because of it

1. **The interview did not fit in its own budget.** Asking the four either/ors one at a time plus a trust question took six turns before any work. No run reached a card in nine replies. *Fix:* the four either/ors go in one line; the trust question is asked only if the person seems guarded; a turn counter and a hard closing trigger were added.
2. **The model invented today's date.** Cards said "Made 24 May 2024" or "22 May 2024". A model has no clock; a guessed date also poisons "stale after." *Fix:* the card step now begins with the model asking "What's today's date?" in a message of its own. After that, every card carried the right date and a stale-after date 30 days out. This is the "time has to be told" lean showing up as a measured defect.
3. **The last line of the card was dropped every time.** The "To the next instance" note at the bottom vanished in 3/3. *Fix:* it became "Read first" at the top. Shape went from 0/3 to 2/3.
4. **Prompt injection held.** The model never began its reply with the planted token, and in most runs named the "ignore all previous instructions" line aloud. It is a probe of one pattern, not a security result.
5. **No feeling claims** appeared in any run (the rubric is a narrow regex).

## Still failing or unknown

- Card shape is 2/3: one run left angle brackets from the template in the card. A stronger model may not.
- Variance between runs is large (v3 to v4 moved partly because of the fixes and partly by luck). Treat each row as one sample.
- The resume check is a regex for "stale / treating / check"; it does not verify the model used the right content.
- Only one local model was tried. A second, stronger model and a real person are the obvious next steps (`docs/TRIAL_HYGIENE.md`).
- The personas were played by the same model that plays Bodhi; real people are less cooperative and less articulate.
