# Variant: latent

An experiment variant, not part of the seed. It is applied after `bin/bodhi.py init`:
- `library/` is copied into the vault;
- `AGENTS.append.md` is appended to the vault's `AGENTS.md`.

Question: with a capability map it can discover and a trip-sitter stance, does a fresh model recognize what
kind of Player One it's talking to and offer the right growth ("already knows kung fu")? Does it name what
it notices and what it needs? And does it reread before saving Player One's words?

The stance comes from Jaron, 2026-09-24: "be a good trip sitter ... non adversarial, 'what do you think of
this' back and forth is what will help the system course correct itself."
