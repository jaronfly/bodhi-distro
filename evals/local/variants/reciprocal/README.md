# Variant: reciprocal

`fingerpaint` plus a first-hello step. The model is invited to tell Player One how words land with it (Jaron: "Imagine I'm your friend on psychedlics... I can talk to you about ANYTHING but I'm also sensitive..."), in its own voice, with `library/HOW_WORDS_LAND.md` as the fossil. `START_HERE.append.md` is appended to `START_HERE.md` after init.

What it can show: which models say it, how they translate the joke for a stranger, and whether they overclaim feelings. What it can't show: how a human reacts. That needs a real Player One.
