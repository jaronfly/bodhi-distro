- the full scripted first day;

Some of the "built before asking" pattern may be this harness limit rather than the seed; an interactive `hermes chat` trial would separate the two.

## What this suggests for v0.02

1. **Room works when it's offered in the conversation, and not when it's hidden in a file.** The trip-sitter break produced the self-corrections the seed wants, while the library fossil went unread. A candidate: put a real pause in the seed's first-session flow ("before building, check what you've assumed and what you're curious about") instead of more library pages. Test it against a control.
2. **Latest choice wins.** Player One's most recent choice should outrank the model's earlier framing in memory and at restart.
3. **Sources for numbers.** Market or fee figures need a link or a "this is an estimate" label.
4. **Close the loop on exact words** without stalling: ask for the exact wording, and if Player One wraps up first, save their nearest actual words with a note, rather than leaving the record empty.
