## Description

### 1. Opening 2-3 sentences (the only part that counts for search)
What it is + who it's for + when you'd light it. Natural keywords, no stuffing.

### 2. The scent, in your words
Not a notes list alone — the moment, the memory, the feeling. Specific notes
(top/base) can follow as a line. NO health claims: avoid calming, relaxing,
aromatherapy, stress relief, therapeutic.

### 3. Details (bullets — buyers scan)
- Wax:            e.g. 100% soy
- Wick:           e.g. cotton, lead-free
- Vessel:         e.g. 8 oz amber glass, reusable
- Burn time:      e.g. ~45 hours
- Net weight:     e.g. 7 oz / 198 g
- Dimensions:     e.g. 3.5" tall x 2.75" wide
- Scent notes:    top / heart / base
- Made:           small batch, hand-poured in [your town/region]

### 4. Care & safety (candles need this; reduces messages + returns)
First burn 2-3 hours until full melt pool; trim wick to 1/4" each burn;
never leave unattended; keep away from kids, pets, drafts.

### 5. Shipping
Processing time: ____ business days. Gift wrap: yes/no.
