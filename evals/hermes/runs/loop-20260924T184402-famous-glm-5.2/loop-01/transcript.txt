1. Posting "whenever you feel like it." Every video on a small account gets tested on a small batch of viewers, and the platform decides whether to push it further based on whether those people finish it. Irregular posting means the system never learns who your audience is, so every video starts cold. Multiple studies land on the same sweet spot: 3-5 posts a week. Not 20 — three good ones a week beats daily mediocre ones. But three every week, same rhythm.

For small accounts, completion rate is nearly everything: a 20-second video that people finish beats a 60-second one they abandon. The pattern that works in your exact niche: flash the "before" for 2 seconds, fast transformation montage, payoff reveal — with bold on-screen text the whole way, because most people watch on mute. Raw and handheld outperforms polished here; the proven formats are before/after speed edits, "$10 flip challenge," "POV: found this ugly thing," and haul-plus-vision ("this blazer becomes a corset").

One honest note on "famous": it's a lagging indicator, not a strategy. The controllable version is "videos strangers finish and share." Do that 20 times in a row and fame stops being random.
