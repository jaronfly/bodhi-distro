# Creator growth plan (short-form video)

Workflow that produced Player One's thrift-flip plan (Sept 2026). Reuse for any "nobody watches my videos" request.

## Procedure
1. Ask (or assume + label): platform, content type, face vs hands, worst-week cadence floor. Worst week, not best, sets the cadence.
2. Research the niche: what formats currently work, posting-frequency consensus, and 1-2 case studies of creators in that niche who grew. Distinguish platform guidance from vendor marketing.
3. Write a dated 30-day calendar artifact to the workspace AND copy it to the profile dir (/Users/jaronfly/.hermes/profiles/bodhi-loop-184402-02/) — the /tmp workspace is ephemeral.
4. Define the next checkpoint: analytics after 2 weeks of posting (3-second retention, watch time, saves+shares per 1k views, profile visits). Iterate hook families on that data.

## Convergent findings (2026, short-form)
- Cadence: 3-5 posts/week held for months; compounding is back-loaded (months 4-6 > 1-3), which is when people quit. Judge accounts on 90-day trends.
- Hook: first 2 seconds decide distribution. Cold-open with result, number, or ugliness — never slow intros. For resale/flips: lead with the price spread ("I paid $4, it sells for $80").
- Series beat one-offs: 2-3 repeatable named formats (flagship transformation, process/thinking-out-loud, micro-tutorial). How-to gets ~30% more shares than hauls.
- Batching beats willpower: one filming session + one edit session covers the week; schedule posts so a bad day costs nothing.
- Metrics that matter: 3-second retention, completion, saves+shares, profile-visit conversion — not follower count. Judge a hook family after 5 uses.
- Viral moment protocol: if a video crosses ~10K views in 48h, within 24h post a follow-up answering the top comment, pin an either/or question, tease the next installment.
