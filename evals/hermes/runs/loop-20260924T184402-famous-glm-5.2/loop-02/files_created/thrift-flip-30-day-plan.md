# Thrift Flip — 30-Day Growth Plan

Assumptions (correct any and the plan regenerates):
- Main platform: TikTok. Same vertical cut cross-posted to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- You sew/upcycle the clothing yourself.
- Mix of on-camera and hands-and-garment footage.
- Floor cadence: 3 posts/week on your worst week. Scale to 4 in good weeks, never below 3.

Posting slots: Monday ~6pm, Wednesday ~6pm, Saturday ~11am (your local time).
Refine these after 2 weeks using your analytics' "when your viewers are online."

---

## THE FORMAT SYSTEM (3 repeatable series — fame comes from series, not one-offs)

- FLIP #N — The flagship. 20-30s. Cold open on the price + the ugliness. Fast montage. Reveal. On-camera for at least the reveal (face builds parasocial attachment; hands-only caps it).
- SOURCE WITH ME — "Thinking out loud" at the store. You pick things up, explain, REJECT some. This was the single highest-performing pivot in the case research: filming decisions, not just finds.
- MICRO-TUTORIAL / COMMENT REPLY — 15-45s of pure utility (seam checks, fabric ID, hem hack) or a direct answer to a real comment. How-to content gets ~30% more shares than hauls.

Hashtags per post: 1 broad (#thriftflip) + 2 niche (e.g. #ThriftTok #upcycledfashion). Trending audio where it fits the vibe, never forced.

---

## WEEK 1 — Establish the format
1. Mon — FLIP #1. Hook: "This $4 muumuu is about to become my favorite summer dress."
2. Wed — SOURCE WITH ME #1. Hook: "I found the perfect blazer today. Then I checked the seams and put it back — here's why."
3. Sat — MICRO-TUTORIAL. Hook: "3 things I check before buying ANY thrifted clothing (the third one saved me $100)."

## WEEK 2 — Repeat the format, vary the subject
4. Mon — FLIP #2. Hook: "It's a XXL men's shirt. In 30 seconds it's a two-piece set."
5. Wed — SOURCE WITH ME #2. Hook: "Everything I almost bought today and why I said no (the $1 rack trap)."
6. Sat — STYLING. Hook: "One thrifted blazer, three outfits, $12 total."
7. (Optional 4th) — COMMENT REPLY. Hook: "You asked how I knew it was real linen. Here's the 5-second test."

## WEEK 3 — Stakes and vulnerability
8. Mon — FLIP #3 with stakes. Hook: "I have 24 hours to turn this $5 disaster into something I'd actually wear."
9. Wed — SOURCE WITH ME #3. Hook: "The 5 items everyone walks past that are actually money."
10. Sat — MICRO-TUTORIAL. Hook: "How to hem pants with no sewing machine — $0, 10 minutes."
11. (Optional 4th) — FAILS. Hook: "Three thrift flips I ruined and what they taught me." (Self-deprecation drives shares; perfection doesn't.)

## WEEK 4 — Consolidate + close the loop
12. Mon — FLIP #4, nostalgia angle. Hook: "I'm turning my dad's old ___ into something I'll wear every week."
13. Wed — MONTH RECAP. Hook: "Everything I flipped this month: total cost, total hours, what it's actually worth."
14. Sat — COMMENT REPLY / Q&A. Hook: "The question I get most: 'how do you know it'll look good on you?'"

## BACKLOG (swap in for missed slots or low-energy weeks)
15. "I only had $20 at the thrift store. Here's what I did with it." (constraint format)
16. "Thrift store red flags: 4 things I never buy used."
17. "Turning one ugly skirt into two new pieces — zero-waste flip."
18. "You voted in the comments: I'm flipping the ___ you picked." (poll-driven; use once audience exists)

Swap rule: if you resell as-is rather than sewing, the FLIP series becomes "bought for $X, sold for $Y" sold-story videos — same hooks, same calendar.

---

## BATCHING (the system that survives your worst week — ~4.5 h/week total)

- Sunday (2h): thrift run WITH the phone out. Film 20-30 min of thinking-out-loud footage = 2 SOURCE videos. Buys the week's stock.
- Tuesday (90 min): transform while filming. Capture 2 before/afters.
- Thursday (60-75 min): one edit session, cut all 3, schedule Mon/Wed/Sat on all 3 platforms. Done.
- Daily (10 min): reply to EVERY comment with real answers. Log questions — each one is a future slot.

Never film one video at a time. A bad Tuesday costs you nothing because the week already exists.

---

## METRICS (review every 2 weeks, 20 minutes — not daily, not by vibes)

Track per video:
1. Hook rate — retention past the first 3 seconds. Most viewers gone before your reveal = first-frame problem, not an algorithm problem.
2. Completion / avg watch time on the 20-30s videos.
3. Saves + shares per 1,000 views — this is the distribution currency.
4. Profile visits → follower conversion.

Rules:
- Judge hook FAMILIES after 5 uses, not single videos. Kill the weakest, double the winner.
- Judge the ACCOUNT on 90-day trend, never one post. Months 4-6 usually outperform months 1-3 combined — that's when everyone quits.
- First milestone: 1,000 followers (~2-3 months at this cadence). Unlocks LIVE, which is where resellers actually sell.

## ECHO PROTOCOL (the moment something hits)
If any video crosses ~10K views in 48h:
1. Within 24h, post a follow-up answering the top comment.
2. Pin a comment with a specific either/or question ("Flip the leather jacket or the prom dress next?").
3. Tease the next FLIP in that video, not a generic "follow me."

The video is the spark. This protocol is what turns a spark into an audience.
