## SUNDAY RUN SHEET (the 2-hour thrift run)

Before you leave:
- [ ] Phone storage cleared, battery full, horizontal DO NOT — vertical 9:16 all day.
- [ ] A cheap phone grip or prop the cart for hands-free moments.
- [ ] One sentence memorized for every clip's first 3 seconds (below). Cold opens,
      never "hey guys, so today I thought maybe..."

Film in this order:
1. ENTRY CLIP (30s, for Sat's tutorial intro later — optional): walk in, say the
   constraint: "I have $20 and one hour. Let's see what's actually worth fixing."
2. SOURCE footage (~20-30 min total, phone propped or held low):
   - Pick UP at least 6 items. For each, one take of thinking out loud:
     "Ooh — wool blazer, lining's intact, but the shoulders are 1988 corporate.
     That's a re-cut, not a flip... putting it back."
   - You MUST reject at least 3 on camera. The rejections are the content.
   - Say prices out loud as you check tags. Numbers are hooks.
3. THE KEEPER: when you find the piece you'll actually flip, film the moment
   you decide. "This. This is the one. Four dollars. Here's the plan: ..."
   State the transformation plan on camera NOW — it becomes Monday's hook.

Buy only what you flagged on camera. Budget cap: $20.

While you're there, grab (for Sat's tutorial, $0 extra):
- [ ] One garment with a busted seam or missing button (teaching example).
- [ ] Check tags on 5 items: note fabric content (linen/cotton/poly) for the
      "how I know it's real linen" beat.

---

## TUESDAY TRANSFORM CHECKLIST (90 min, film while you work)

- [ ] BEFORE shot: garment flat, good light, 5 seconds still. Then one detail
      pan (the ugly part the hook names).
- [ ] Film the FIRST cut — scissors meeting fabric is the "no going back" beat.
- [ ] 2-3 process clips, 10-20s each, no narration needed (voice-over later).
- [ ] THE REVEAL: worn, on camera, full body, 5+ seconds of you actually
      reacting — don't perform it, wear it and look in the mirror.
- [ ] The price card, handwritten, held to camera: "$4 → this."

## THURSDAY EDIT & SCHEDULE (60-75 min, one sitting)

Cut 3 videos, schedule all 3 platforms (TikTok first, then Reels, Shorts):
1. MON 6pm — FLIP #1 (20-30s). Hook: "This $4 [item] is about to become
   my favorite [garment]." Cold open on the price tag or the ugliness.
2. WED 6pm — SOURCE WITH ME #1. Hook: "I found the perfect [item] today.
   Then I checked the seams and put it back — here's why."
3. SAT 11am — MICRO-TUTORIAL. Hook: "3 things I check before buying ANY
   thrifted clothing (the third one saved me $100)."

Per post: 1 broad hashtag (#thriftflip) + 2 niche (#ThriftTok #upcycledfashion).
Trending audio only if it fits the vibe.

Post all 3. Ugly is fine. Late is fine (that's what scheduling is for).
Missing a slot is the only failure mode.

After Saturday's post: the 2-week clock starts. First analytics check-in
~Oct 8: 3-second retention, completion, saves+shares per 1,000 views,
