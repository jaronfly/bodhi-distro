# Candle listing template

One file per product. Fill what's known; mark the rest UNKNOWN and fix later.

## Anatomy (order matters — first two lines show in search previews)

1. TITLE — keywords first. Pattern:
   [Scent Name] Candle | [wax type] | [scent family words] | Hand Poured | [oz]oz [vessel]
   Etsy: ~140 chars, front-load what people search. Avoid: "beautiful", "amazing".

2. OPENING HOOK (2 lines max)
   What it smells like + who it's for. Their voice, not ad voice.

3. SCENT STORY
   Top / heart / base notes if known, or a plain-language description.
   Evocative but concrete. No "elevated olfactory journey" nonsense.

4. THE OBJECT — specs block, same on every listing:
   - Size: __ oz
   - Burn time: ~__ hours
   - Wax: __ (soy / coconut / blend)
   - Wick: __ (cotton / wood)
   - Vessel: __ (amber glass / tin / etc.)
   - Fragrance: phthalate-free? / essential oils? / fragrance oils?
   - Hand poured in ___, in small batches

5. CARE & SAFETY (same every listing)
   First burn 2-3 hours until full melt pool. Trim wick to 1/4" before
   each burn. Never leave a burning candle unattended.

6. PROCESSING / SHIPPING
   Made to order? Ships in __ business days.

7. TAGS (Etsy: 13 tags, 20 chars each)
   scent name variants, scent family, gift terms, room terms, occasion
