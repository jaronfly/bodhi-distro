# Voice + Product Interview — fill in, delete lines you hate, leave blanks freely

Player One: answer in your own words, messy is fine. Short answers beat polished ones.
Everything here is raw material for listings. Nothing gets published unedited.

## PART A — Voice (answer once, works for all 12 products)

A1. How did this shop start? Two or three sentences, the way you'd tell a neighbor.

A2. Finish this: "I make candles because ______."

A3. What do customers thank you for most, in reviews or messages? Quote one if you can.

A4. What do you REFUSE to do that bigger candle brands do? (paraffin, imported,
    mass-produced, mystery fragrance loads, whatever it is — this is usually
    where your identity actually lives)

A5. How do you describe scents to a friend: by ingredients (cedar, sage),
    by mood (cozy, clean), or by memory/place (campfire, grandma's kitchen)?
    Pick the one that feels most you — or say "depends."

A6. Three words your best friend would use to describe YOUR CANDLES (not you).

A7. Anything about how you make them that would surprise a customer?

A8. How formal are you in customer messages? Give one example line you've
    actually written (greeting or sign-off style).

## PART B — Product sheet (copy per candle, one block each)

Product: <name as it appears in your shop>
B1. Scent name and what it ACTUALLY smells like — plain words, be honest
    (e.g. "cedar shavings, fresh sage, a little dirt. Not sweet.")
B2. Why does this scent exist? (a memory, a request, an experiment that worked)
B3. Vessel + size (8oz amber jar, etc.), wax type, wick type
B4. Burn time if you know it; weight if you don't
B5. Price
B6. Who buys this one? Gift or self-treat? What occasion?
B7. Anything annoying/special about making it?
