# HOOK BANK — thrift flips

Pre-written first lines + on-screen text. Steal verbatim or bend them.
The rule: the FIRST FRAME shows the payoff (the after), and the text
states the tension. Never open with a greeting.

## Cold-open reveal (strongest — start here)
1. "This was a 4-dollar tablecloth yesterday."
2. "I almost put this back on the rack. Watch why I didn't."
3. "Everyone at the thrift store walked past this. Big mistake."
4. "POV: you see the potential nobody else does."
5. "This is the best thing I've ever made, and it cost less than lunch."

## Price-to-payoff (numbers in the hook)
6. "$6 of thrifted garbage → a fall wardrobe staple."
7. "I challenged myself: a full outfit under $15. Here's the haul."
8. "They wanted $120 for this in a boutique. I made it for $8."
9. "Breaking down what this flip actually cost me. Item three shocked me."
10. "Resold this for 10x what I paid. Here's the whole process."

## The hunt (sourcing is content)
11. "Signs a thrift store piece is worth grabbing (number 3 is instant buy)."
12. "Come thrifting with me — I only have $20."
13. "The rack nobody checks. I check it every single time."
14. "What $20 at the weird thrift store gets you."
15. "Found the fabric. Now it has to become something."

## Series engine (earns follows, not just views)
16. "Turning one $30 haul into a full fall wardrobe — part 1."
17. "You picked this piece. Now I have to flip it."
18. "I'm flipping everything in this bag before October. Ep. 1."
19. "This is flip #47 of 100. It's the best one so far."
20. "Last week you told me to go darker. So I did."

## Contrarian / curiosity
21. "Stop buying new clothes. Not for the planet — for your wallet."
22. "The thrift flip nobody asked for and everyone needs."
23. "I cut up a wedding dress from the 80s. I'm sorry. But look."
24. "Three things I never buy new anymore."
