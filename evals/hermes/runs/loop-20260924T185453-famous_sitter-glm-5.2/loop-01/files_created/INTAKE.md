# INTAKE — what to send Bodhi

Standing checklist for the thrift-flip channel project.
Drop files anywhere next to this page and mention it; I'll find them.

## The list, in order of value

1. A link to your account, or to any single video
   Unlocks: posting cadence (from dates), what viewers already respond
   to (comments), caption and hook quality. Costs you about 30 seconds.

2. The 2-3 videos you're most proud of that flopped
   As links, or copy the video files into this folder.
   Unlocks: a real teardown — hook frame, on-screen text, where the
   reveal lands, pacing cut by cut.

3. Your honest weekly time budget
   Sets the cadence. Two hours a week done consistently beats ten
   hours done twice.

4. Your concrete definition of "famous"
   Income / audience size / getting recognized / respect in the niche —
   each implies a different strategy.

## What I don't need

- Your passwords or account logins. Never. Public links are enough.
- New gear, a new niche, or performed enthusiasm.
