# 4-WEEK STARTER CALENDAR — 8 posts, one 2-hour batch shoot a week

Assumption: 2-3 hrs/week, TikTok-first, two posts a week on days you
pick and keep (pick once, then never think about it again).

## Weekly rhythm
- One 2-hour batch shoot: film ALL footage for that week's 2 posts.
- Cut each video to 20-35 seconds. First frame = the payoff.
- Post, then reply to every comment within the first hour.

## Week 1 — Cold-open reveals (HOOK_BANK 1-5)
- Post 1: your single best existing flip, re-cut: after first, 3-second
  hook text, story compressed to under 30s.
- Post 2: a second flip, same structure. (You're teaching the algorithm
  what this account is.)
- Batch shot list: finished pieces on a hanger rack, 4-6 angles each,
  "before" photos if you have them, 10s of hands working per piece.

## Week 2 — Price-to-payoff (hooks 6-10)
- Post 3: a haul → total spend on screen the whole video.
- Post 4: one flip with a full cost breakdown (item, thread, time).
- Batch shot list: tags/price stickers macro shots, receipt, fabric
  close-ups, the worn result.

## Week 3 — The hunt (hooks 11-15)
- Post 5: thrift-store walkthrough, voiceover of what you're scanning for.
- Post 6: "$20 at the weird store" haul video.
- Batch shot list: this week's shoot happens AT the store. Phone clips:
  rack pans, the grab moment, tag close-ups.

## Week 4 — Series launch (hooks 16-20)
- Post 7: part 1 of a multi-part flip. End on an open loop:
  "part 2 is the reveal."
- Post 8: part 2. Pin a comment linking back to part 1.
- Batch shot list: whatever the series needs, shot with the ending in mind.

## After week 4
Open TRACKER.csv, find the two formats with the best completion/rewatch,
and double down on those in month two. That's the whole strategy:
let the audience pick, not the mood.
