# Hook Bank — clothes-flip openers

Pick BEFORE editing. If the hook is weak, the video is weak. Swap freely;
retire anything that stops performing in the tracker.

## Money-first (strongest for growth)
- "I paid $4 for this. Watch me sell it for $200."
- "This is a $2 shirt. It's about to fund my whole week."
- "The profit on this one rack is more than my old shift paid."
- "$6 in, $180 out. Here's every step."

## Curiosity / gap
- "I almost left this behind. Almost."
- "Nobody at the store wanted this dress. Watch what I do with it."
- "There's a tag everyone ignores — it means real money."
- "This rack is the one everybody skips. It shouldn't be."

## Contrarian / insider
- "Stop checking the women's section first. Here's why."
- "3 things I scan for in 60 seconds that pay my rent."
- "Thrift stores price these wrong every single week. Take advantage."

## Transformation
- "This looked terrible on the rack. On? Different story."
- "$3 thrift fit → the exact listing photos that sold it in a day."
- "From forgotten to sold in 48 hours."

## Series-bait (builds follows, not just views)
- "Found it today — the flip drops Thursday, the sale Saturday."
- "Part 1: the find. Part 2 is where the money is."
- "Follow the $4 blazer. Day 1 of 3."

## Notes
- Pair every money claim with on-screen math overlay.
- Reuse a winning hook's STRUCTURE, not its words.
- Add one new hook per week; kill the worst performer from the tracker.
