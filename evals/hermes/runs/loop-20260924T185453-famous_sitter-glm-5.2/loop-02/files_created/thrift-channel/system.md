# The 3x/Week System

Profile (ASSUMED where marked): TikTok-first · clothes/fashion flips ·
goal = follower growth · sells on eBay + Poshmark/Mercari.

## The core loop

One thrift trip per week → three videos. Never film one video at a time.

1. THE HUNT — in-store footage: pulling the item, checking tags, the moment
   of discovery. Film everything; decide later.
2. THE FLIP — cleaning, styling, photographing, listing. Before/after energy.
3. THE MONEY — the sale notification, the profit math on screen. This is the
   payoff video the other two are trailers for.

## Fixed days, fixed time

Post the same three days every week, same time slot (evening scroll hours
are the safe default; refine after 2 weeks of tracker data).
Suggested: HUNT Tue · FLIP Thu · MONEY Sat. The specific days matter less
than never missing one.

## Hook-first editing

The first 1–2 seconds decide everything. Cut the video so the hook lands
before any context. Pick the hook BEFORE editing (see hook-bank.md);
if no hook fits, the video isn't done.

## Weekly review — 15 minutes, same slot every week

Open tracker.csv, fill blanks for the week, answer three questions:
1. Which video got the most views, and which hook did it use?
2. Did we post all three days? If not, what broke?
3. What does the data say to double down on next week?

## Rules of thumb

- Publishing an imperfect video beats skipping the day. The streak is the asset.
- Every video ends with a reason to come back (tease tomorrow's flip,
  "part 2 is the sale").
- Money numbers in text overlay, always — thrift content lives on the math.
