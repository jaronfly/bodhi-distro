# Etsy listing skeleton — current Etsy guidance (verified Sep 2026)

Etsy's search now reads the WHOLE listing (title, tags, attributes,
description, photos, reviews) — not just the title. So titles should be
clear and human; descriptions carry keywords naturally; tags cover angles
the title doesn't.

## Title (max 140 chars)
- Front-load what the item IS: "Wild Fig & Cedar Scented Candle"
- One clear noun phrase, then secondary phrases separated by commas/pipes
- NO subjective words (beautiful, perfect) — move to description
- NO sale/shipping info, no repeated words, no shop name
- Under ~80 chars is fine if it's clear; first ~40 chars matter most

## Description
1. FIRST 160 CHARS carry the weight (mobile preview + Google). One or two
   sentences: what it is + top keyword + a human hook. Never copy the title.
2. The scent, in plain spoken words (from voice capture #1)
3. Details as bullets — size, wax, wick, burn time, vessel, weight
4. The maker: 2-3 sentences of the real story, first person ("I"), not "we"
5. Gifting angle if relevant (from voice capture #3)
6. Care + safety, brief: first burn 2 hours, trim wick, don't leave burning

## Tags — 13 slots, max 20 chars each
- Don't repeat title phrases; cover NEW angles
- Mix: 2-3 broad (soy candle), rest long-tail (candle gift for her,
  fall candle, wood wick candle, housewarming gift)
- Attributes (color, occasion, scent type) are free keyword slots — fill them

## Photos (for later): first photo = the product clear and bright;
later photos show scale (hand next to jar), lit wick, label detail, packaging.
