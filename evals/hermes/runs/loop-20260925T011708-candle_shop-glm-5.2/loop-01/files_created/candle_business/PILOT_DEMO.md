# Pilot listing — DEMO VOICES (assumed facts, not yours yet)

Same candle, written three ways so you can point at a direction instead of
describing one. Every fact below is an ASSUMPTION (labeled) standing in
until your real ones arrive from VOICE_CAPTURE.md.

Assumed facts: 9 oz soy candle, cotton wick, ~50 hr burn, "Wild Fig & Cedar"
scent, $28, poured in small batches. Assumed buyer split: mostly
self-treat, some gifting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
## A — PLAIN SPOKEN (bakery-that-sells-firewood energy)

Title: Wild Fig & Cedar Scented Candle, 9 oz Soy candle, Woodsy Fall Candle

First 160: A woodsy, slightly sweet soy candle that smells like fig trees
at the edge of a cedar grove. Poured by hand in small batches, in a glass
jar you'll actually reuse.

Scent, spoken: Fig comes in first — jammy, a little sweet, like fruit
drying in the sun. Cedar follows and takes it darker, like a pencil
shaving's nicer cousin. Together it reads "outside, but the good parts."

Details:
- 9 oz soy wax, cotton wick, ~50 hour burn
- Hand-poured in small batches
- Reusable glass vessel
- $28

From my bench: I started pouring candles in my kitchen because I couldn't
find one that smelled like actual things instead of "autumn ambiance."
This was the third scent I made and the one I keep for myself.

## B — WARM & PERSONAL (letter-to-a-friend energy)

Title: Wild Fig & Cedar Candle, Small Batch Soy Candle, Housewarming Gift

First 160: The candle I make when I want my apartment to smell like a
place I'd like to visit. Sweet fig, dry cedar, a 50-hour burn, poured one
small batch at a time.

Scent, spoken: Think of the last warm afternoon of September. That's what
I was chasing — fig's late sweetness over cedar that's more forest than
closet. My first tester burned through in two weeks because I kept
"checking" it.

Details: 9 oz soy / cotton wick / ~50 hr / reusable jar / $28

From my bench: Every candle I pour starts on my kitchen counter, usually
after the kid's asleep. If it doesn't make the whole room smell right in
ten minutes, it gets re-melted and tried again. This one passed on the
second try.

## C — DRY & DIRECT (spec-sheet-with-a-pulse energy)

Title: Wild Fig & Cedar Soy Candle, 9 oz, 50 Hour Burn, Hand Poured

First 160: Fig and cedar, no perfume-counter sweetness. Hand-poured soy
candle, 9 ounces, about 50 hours of burn. Made in batches of thirty or
fewer.

Scent, spoken: Sweet fig up front, cedar underneath. Not a bakery candle,
not a cologne candle. If you like your scents on the darker side of
woody, this one's yours.

Details: 9 oz soy / cotton wick / ~50 hr / reusable jar / $28

From my bench: I test every scent in my own living room before it gets a
label. This one lasted three versions before it earned its jar.
