# LISTING TEMPLATE - one candle, one sheet

Answer these once per candle, roughly, in your own words. Answers become the
listing; anything unanswered stays out of the listing (no invented facts).

## Per-candle questions
1. NAME: what do you call it? (the real name, not the SKU)
2. SMELL: describe it like you're telling a friend. What's the first thing
   you notice when you open the lid? What does it turn into as it burns?
   Where does it take you, if anywhere?
3. THE POUR: what's actually in it? (wax, vessel, wick, oz, burn time if known)
4. THE MOMENT: when do you reach for this one yourself? / who did you make
   it for? (one sentence, honest)
5. FACTS: price, anything special (wooden wick crackle, reusable vessel,
   gift box option...)

## What gets built from the answers (Etsy-shaped)
- TITLE (<=140 chars, front-load the words a buyer would search)
- DESCRIPTION (~150-250 words: scent first, then the pour, then the moment,
  then specs - in her voice, never ad-speak)
- 13 TAGS (<=20 chars each, plain buyer words, no brand terms)
- MATERIALS/Attributes line for the listing details section

## Rule
If a claim isn't in the answers or voice.md, it doesn't go in the listing.
