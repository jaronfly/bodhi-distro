The short version of what I am: I grow around the work you choose. You bring the task or the question — writing, planning, research, a project, a passing idea — and I do the useful part of it with the tools this machine actually has (shell, files, web, browser), then tell you plainly what happened and what's still uncertain.

So, three questions to aim with:

1. What do you want AI to help change or make?
2. What would a useful first win look like — something small enough to finish today?
3. If that's too abstract: what do you wish were easier?

One real example grounds this better than a setup tour, so if you've got a task or a note sitting around, throw it at me.
